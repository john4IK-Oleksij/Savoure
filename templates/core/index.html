What’s on Your Mind?

What are the restaurant's hours of operation?
Our restaurant is open daily from 12:00 PM to 10:30 PM. We strive to provide a welcoming atmosphere throughout the entire day, whether you're joining us for lunch or dinner.
Do I need to make a reservation before coming?
Reservations are not strictly required, but they are highly recommended during peak evening hours and weekends to guarantee your table.

Yes, our seasonal menu features dedicated vegetarian and plant-forward gourmet creations prepared with the same artistry as our classic French repertoire.
